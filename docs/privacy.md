# CursorFocus Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

CursorFocus doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

**Key presses.** The App Store version doesn't look at the keyboard at all.
The version that redirects typing looks at each key press to decide whether
it should go to the window under the pointer. A key press is held for a
fraction of a second while that window comes forward, then delivered to it.
Key presses are never written to disk, logged or sent anywhere.

**Windows.** CursorFocus reads the position and size of windows on screen to
work out which one is under the pointer. It doesn't read window titles or
contents.

**Settings.** Your preferences, including the apps you exclude, are stored on
your Mac only.

**Network.** CursorFocus makes no network connections. There are no
analytics, crash reporters or ads.
